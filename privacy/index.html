GCOB Prompt Helper / Policy

Privacy policy.

Last updated: 26 October 2026 · Publisher: jGuru

GCOB Prompt Helper does not add data collection or send your information to the publisher or an external service beyond ChatGPT.

1. Scope

This policy describes the GCOB Prompt Helper plugin published by jGuru and this informational website. It distinguishes the helper’s behavior from the services provided by ChatGPT and GitHub.

2. Information handled by the helper

The helper uses the prompt and clarification answers you provide in your ChatGPT conversation to refine that prompt. The publisher does not collect, receive, store, sell, or share that conversation through a separate backend, analytics system, or external integration. The helper has no publisher-operated data store or MCP service.

3. ChatGPT remains the host

Your conversation is processed within ChatGPT. OpenAI’s applicable policies, your account settings, and any workspace agreement govern that processing, retention, and deletion. This policy does not replace them. See OpenAI’s privacy policy and the policies applicable to your region and account.

4. Choosing Run

Prompt refinement itself requires no external services. If you explicitly choose Run, ChatGPT may use available tools to complete the task you requested. Any resulting access or transmission follows that task, the connected service’s terms, and ChatGPT’s permissions. This is distinct from publisher-side collection by the helper.

5. This website

The site contains static pages, locally served styles and a local icon. It uses no analytics scripts, tracking pixels, advertising, forms, cookies, or browser storage added by jGuru. It does not accept prompts for processing.

When hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub handles website requests and may process technical request information under its own privacy statement. The absence of publisher-added tracking does not mean the hosting provider processes no data.

6. Voluntary support contact

If you choose to contact jGuru through GitHub, GitHub processes what you submit and the publisher can see that submission. Public issues are visible to others. Share a minimal, anonymized example rather than private conversation content. See the support page.

7. Retention and deletion

There is no separate publisher-side prompt database to retain or delete. Manage ChatGPT conversations through ChatGPT and your workspace settings. GitHub handles content submitted to its own services under its policies and controls.

8. Questions and updates

Use the support page for questions about this policy. Changes to the helper’s data handling will be reflected here with an updated date.
